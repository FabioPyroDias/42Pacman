# Project Management - Pacman

## Overview

This document outlines the structured project management approach adopted for the Pacman project.

It compiles all documentation from the repository's dedicated management directory, including the project timeline, actual progress tracking, technical analysis and key choices, risk management and mitigation strategies, team organization and decision-making workflows, acceptance test plans, and a comprehensive record of development roadblocks and conflict resolution.

Developed by a two person team over a three-week period.
